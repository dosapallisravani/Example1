 # AgriCraft Connect 🌾

### AI-Powered Market Linkage for Rural Producers

AgriCraft Connect is a Smart India Hackathon prototype designed to connect rural producers, artisans, and buyers through AI-assisted product cataloging and smart market matching.

The platform allows rural producers to list products using a photo and local-language voice input. Buyers can share their requirements, and the system helps identify suitable products and producers.

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Rural artisans and small producers often face difficulties such as:

- Limited access to wider markets
- Difficult and time-consuming digital product listing
- Language and typing barriers
- Uncertain product pricing
- Lack of direct connections with suitable buyers
- Dependence on middlemen and physical markets

These challenges reduce their market opportunities and income potential.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

AgriCraft Connect uses AI-assisted tools to simplify product listing and connect rural producers with the right buyers.

The platform provides:

- Photo-based product listing
- Local-language voice input
- AI-generated product descriptions
- Product classification and smart tags
- Price and demand insights
- Buyer requirement analysis
- AI-based seller–buyer matching
- Request to Buy functionality
- Accept, Reject, and Negotiate options
- Voice verification simulation
- Digital order receipt generation
