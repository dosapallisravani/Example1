ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology with a Rural Purpose

AgriCraft Connect is an AI-powered market linkage prototype designed to help rural producers connect with suitable buyers through simple photo and voice-based interaction.

Our goal is to reduce digital barriers, improve product discovery and support better market access.
